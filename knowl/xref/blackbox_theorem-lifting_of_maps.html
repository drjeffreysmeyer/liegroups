Blackbox Theorem 1.6.6 Lifting of Maps.

If \(X\) is a topological space, \(\pi: \tilde{X} \to X\) a covering map, \(Y\) is a path-connected, locally path-connected space, and \(f:Y\to X\) is continuous map, then there exists a lift \(\tilde{f}: Y \to \tilde{X}\) such that \(f = \pi \circ \tilde{f}\) if and only if
\begin{equation*} f_* (\pi_1(Y)) \subset \pi_*(\pi_1(\tilde{X})). \end{equation*}
